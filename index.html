Our 1958 Architectural Heritage by Victor A. Lundy

Originally opened on March 16, 1958 as the Warm Mineral Springs Inn, our landmark property was designed by celebrated modernist architect Victor A. Lundy, renowned for his graceful curves and signature "roofs that soar."

AIA Florida "Official Journal" • August 1958 Issue
“I was searching for a form that would somehow symbolize the thought of the ‘Fountain of Youth’ by a plastic flowing shape, that would also echo the organic growing shape of a tree. The answer came in the adoption of a structural system based on using precast concrete hyperbolic paraboloids, 14'-5" square (from basic motel unit width requirements), plus necessary overlap in two heights arranged in a U-shaped plan... Three of the so called shells were lifted up on higher columns of varying height to form the sign of the motel, as a free-standing piece of sculpture.”
— Victor A. Lundy, Architect
Selected by the American Institute of Architects for the 1958 Special Award
"Champagne Glass" Canopy Roofs
1958

Grand Opening & 10,000 Visitors

Built originally as an ultra-modern 35-unit "L"-shaped inn for a couple from Venice, FL. Its March 16, 1958 open house drew an estimated 10,000 visitors marveling at the mushroom-like champagne glass roofs and glass walls that let guests gaze at the stars from bed.

1985

U-Shaped Expansion & Pool Oasis

The right wing was added to complete the signature U-shaped courtyard layout we enjoy today, alongside the iconic diamond motel sign and the 1985 addition of our tropical courtyard swimming pool and spa area.

Today

Original 1958 Reception Desk & Spirit

While modern updates ensure contemporary comfort for returning guests year after year, we proudly preserve treasures from 1958—including our curved solid wood reception desk in the lobby, truly one of a kind.
